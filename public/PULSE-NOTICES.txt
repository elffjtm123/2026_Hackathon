Camera pulse experiment (local research prototype)

Bundled runtime: @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.2.0, Google MediaPipe Authors.
Code repository: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Code license: Apache License 2.0 (copy: /pulse-licenses/mediapipe-Apache-2.0.txt).
Generated runtime copies retain upstream headers. Face Landmarker model is
prepared from the existing local official MediaPipe task asset; consult its
model documentation and terms before redistribution or production use.

POS signal projection: independently implemented from Wang et al.,
Algorithmic Principles of Remote PPG, IEEE TBME 2017,
DOI 10.1109/TBME.2016.2609282. POS remains a separate comparison experiment.

Presentation mode: EfficientPhys, TS-CAN and PhysFormer pretrained on UBFC-rPPG,
and the Motion Matters MA-UBFC TS-CAN weights (current default),
plus the original PURE EfficientPhys comparison weights, from rPPG-Toolbox,
revision b7500b848f84ad7f86e277b4612563b69f4f88f9.
https://github.com/ubicomplab/rPPG-Toolbox
Motion-augmentation research and attribution: https://motion-matters.github.io/
The official weights/structure are converted to an equivalent ONNX graph;
no retraining is performed. Local backend assets are in .models/pulse.
UBFC artifacts are prepared with scripts/prepare_ubfc_pulse.py. Source and
weight SHA-256 checks and original-PyTorch/ONNX equivalence checks are required.
